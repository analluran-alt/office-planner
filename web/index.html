Rules, scan points and how days are chosen

    Two office days a week are required. The first two motorbike days of the remaining week are chosen; past days are skipped. If fewer than two qualify, the latest remaining days stay train days so a later update can still change them. Forecast is refreshed every 3 hours on GitHub; the planner picks it up Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday at 20:30 or with the button.
