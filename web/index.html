Rules, route scan and how the days are chosen

    Two office days a week are required. The first two motorbike days of the remaining week are chosen; days already passed are skipped. If fewer than two qualify, the latest remaining days are kept as train days so a later update can still change them.

    Forecast is refreshed Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday at 20:30, or with the button. Forecasts beyond two days ahead change often; the Tuesday and Thursday runs are there to catch that.
